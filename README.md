# MyHealth AI — Diabetes Companion

A personal health companion for people with diabetes: blood sugar tracking (mmol/L and mg/dL),
medication tracking, food scanning, walking, and weekly health reports.

Live site: https://shirinmakeup-source.github.io/myhealth-ai/

## Features
- Personal Health Card with editable targets (set with your healthcare provider)
- Daily dashboard: blood sugar chart, calories/carbs remaining, medication schedule, steps & distance
- Food Scanner with Persian & Middle Eastern dishes (rice, tahdig, kebab, stews) — clearly labelled demo mode
- Blood sugar, medication and walking logs with history and charts
- Weekly health report — download/print as PDF to share with your doctor
- Installable Progressive Web App (Android & iPhone), works offline for manual tracking once installed

## Privacy
- All records are stored only in the browser on the user's own device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded to this repository or any server.
- Export/Import your data from Reports. Delete everything from Reports at any time.
- No API keys, passwords, credentials or personal health information are stored in this repository.
- Real AI food analysis needs a secure server endpoint with the key held server-side; until then the scanner runs in demo mode and photos are never sent anywhere.
- This app does not provide diagnoses, medication changes or treatment recommendations.
