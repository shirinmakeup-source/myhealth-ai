Install MyHealth AI

Add it to your home screen — no App Store or Google Play needed. Where your browser supports it, it opens full-screen without browser bars.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow up), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. The MyHealth AI heart icon will appear on your home screen.

On Android / Chrome, tap Install App and accept the browser prompt. If no prompt appears, open your browser menu (⋮) and choose “Install app” / “Add to Home screen”.

App installation (PWA)

This app ships a web app manifest (manifest.webmanifest), a service worker file, and custom icons — 192×192, 512×512, maskable 512×512, Apple Touch Icon (180×180) and favicon — all with the heart + heartbeat logo in emerald, white and soft blue. Use the Install App card at the top; controls hide automatically once installed / in standalone mode.

Real AI food analysis and health-app sync (Samsung Health, Google Health Connect, Apple Health) still need a secure server endpoint with the API key held server-side — never in this page. The food scanner therefore runs in a clearly labelled demo mode with manual entry, and photographs are never sent anywhere until you give permission. This hosted version saves your records on this device automatically and works offline for manual tracking once installed.

AI Food Scanner status

Your data in this version

Your records are saved automatically on this device and stay after you close or refresh the app. Use Reports → Export all my data to keep a backup file you control, and Import to restore it. Delete all data anytime from Reports.

MyHealth AI helps you record and review your own information. It does not diagnose, treat, or recommend medication or dosage changes. Always follow your healthcare provider's advice. Nutrition from photos is an estimate — never use it to determine insulin dosage.
